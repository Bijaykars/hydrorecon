## 9. HYDEST (WECS/DHM 1990) + MHSP 1997 — equations recovered

Open sources: *The Geographic Base* vol. 9 (2022), NepJOL, open access —
nepjol.info/index.php/tgb/article/view/55425 (cites DoED "Guidelines for Study of Hydropower
Projects" 2006; WECS/DHM 1990 via lib.icimod.org/record/4202). Cross-checked with
dhakalvivek.com.np/2021/07/design-flood-estimation-in-Nepal.html.

**WECS/DHM 1990 (HYDEST) floods** (A₃₀₀₀ = catchment area below 3,000 m elevation, km²):

- Q₂ = 1.8767 · (A₃₀₀₀ + 1)^0.8737
- Q₁₀₀ = 14.63 · (A₃₀₀₀ + 1)^0.7342
- Q_T = exp(ln Q₂ + S·σ), σ = ln(Q₁₀₀/Q₂)/2.326
- S = 1.282, 1.645, 2.054, 2.326, 2.576, 2.787 for T = 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500 yr

**Modified HYDEST (DHM 2004)**: Q₂ = 2.29·A₃₀₀₀^0.86, Q₁₀₀ = 20.7·A₃₀₀₀^0.72

**HYDEST mean monthly flows**: Q_month = C · A_total^A1 · (A_below5000m + 1)^A2 · MMP^A3
